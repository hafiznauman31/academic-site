Awarded an official acceptance as a Visiting PhD Scholar at the **College of Fisheries, Huazhong Agricultural University (HZAU)**, Wuhan, China[cite: 2].

Under the proposed academic collaboration scheme of the **HEC CPEC Consortium of Universities**, the prospective 6-month doctoral placement focuses on:
- **Research Scope:** *Behavioral and Transcriptomic Responses of Juvenile Nile Tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) to Environmental Enrichment under Thermal Stress*[cite: 2]
- **Host Lab:** Advanced molecular fish physiology and transcriptomics facilities supervised by Prof. Dr. Zexia Gao[cite: 2].
